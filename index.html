ACC Gold DEMO is an introductory-grade cement product, and DEMO generally stands for a demonstration version or a specific promotional variant used to introduce contractors and builders to the brand's quality. It is designed to offer reliable strength and workability for basic construction needs like masonry work, plastering, and small-scale residential repairs. While it may not possess the heavy-duty performance of premium composite cements meant for massive structural foundations, it provides a cost-effective and dependable option for everyday building tasks.
