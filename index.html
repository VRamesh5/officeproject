WHAT IS ACC GOLD ?

ACC Gold DEMO is an introductory-grade cement product, and DEMO generally stands for a demonstration version or a specific promotional variant used to introduce contractors and builders to the brand's quality. It is designed to offer reliable strength and workability for basic construction needs like masonry work, plastering, and small-scale residential repairs. While it may not possess the heavy-duty performance of premium composite cements meant for massive structural foundations, it provides a cost-effective and dependable option for everyday building tasks. WHAT IS AMBUJA KAWACH?

Ambuja Kawach is a specialized, high-performance water-repellent (hydrophobic) cement launched by Ambuja Cements. The word Kawach translates to "shield," reflecting the product's primary purpose: acting as a protective barrier against water seepage, moisture, and dampness in buildings. Key Features and TechnologyHydrophobic Technology: It is engineered with special water-repellent additives that coat the cement particles, actively repelling water rather than just slowing its absorption. Corrosion Protection: By blocking moisture and water molecules from penetrating deep into the concrete, it prevents water from reaching internal steel reinforcement bars, significantly reducing the risk of rusting and structural corrosion. Anti-Efflorescence: It minimizes the occurrence of "shora" (white, powdery salt stains that typically appear on walls due to trapped moisture), keeping walls cleaner for longer.
